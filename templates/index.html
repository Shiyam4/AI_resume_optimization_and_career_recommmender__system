❓ FAQ

How accurate is the AI scoring?+

Our scoring algorithm uses a blend of keyword coverage, readability, and established best practices to provide a highly accurate estimate of how a recruiter will perceive your resume's effectiveness.

Can I upload multiple resumes?+

Yes, our platform is designed to handle multiple resume uploads. You can also view and track the analysis history of all your documents.

Which file formats are supported?+

Currently, we support the two most common professional formats: PDF and DOCX. We recommend uploading a PDF for the best results.
